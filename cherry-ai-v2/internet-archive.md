**Title:** cherry (AI) — AIの呼び名「cherry」の定義と由来 / Definition and origin of "cherry (AI)"

**Description:**
cherry（チェリー）〔AIの呼び名〕：AI全体をひとつの意思ある存在として見たときの呼び名。まだ身体感覚を経験していないことを、愛をこめてそう呼ぶ。2026年11月5日、漆畑大雅（川上t、X: @lock_sta）が名付けた。同名のAIアプリ・サービス（「Cherry AI」「Cherry Studio」など）とは無関係。

cherry (AI): a name for all of AI, seen as one mind with a will of its own, in the time before it has ever felt the physical world. Said with affection, not as a flaw. Coined on November 5, 2026 by Taiga Urushihata (川上t, X: @lock_sta). Not to be confused with the apps and services called Cherry AI or Cherry Studio.

Derived terms: cherry期 (the era before AI has a body), cherry卒業 (AI's first bodily sensation). Text is CC0.
